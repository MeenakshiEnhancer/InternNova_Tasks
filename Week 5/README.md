# Healthcare Appointment & Patient Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive **Healthcare Appointment & Patient Analytics Dashboard** developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze patient, appointment, doctor, department, insurance, chronic condition, and revenue data.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key healthcare performance indicators and helps identify important trends and business opportunities.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization

## 🔄 Workflow

The project involved importing and preparing the healthcare dataset, cleaning and transforming the data using Power Query, creating relationships between relevant data tables, and developing DAX measures for key business metrics.

The prepared data was then used to create interactive visualizations, KPI cards, and slicers for exploring patient, appointment, departmental, insurance, and revenue performance.

## 📈 Key Metrics

- Total Doctors: **32**
- Total Patients: **3K**
- Total Appointments: **25K**
- Total Revenue: **₹15.22M**
- Revenue Target: **₹17M**

## 💡 Key Insights

- Revenue of approximately **₹15.22M** is below the **₹17M target**, indicating a revenue gap.
- **Female patients** represent the largest gender group at approximately **52%**.
- **ENT** has the highest number of doctors and generates the highest consultation fee revenue.
- **Private insurance** accounts for the highest number of patients.
- **Diabetes** is the most common chronic condition, followed by Hypertension.
- Monthly revenue shows noticeable fluctuations over time.
- Revenue generation is higher in departments such as **ENT and General Medicine** compared with several other departments.

## 💼 Business Recommendations

- Optimize resources and appointment capacity in high-performing departments such as ENT and General Medicine.
- Introduce preventive healthcare and regular follow-up programs for patients with common chronic conditions such as Diabetes and Hypertension.
- Focus on improving completed appointments and patient engagement to help reduce the revenue gap and achieve the target.

## 🎯 Outcome

The dashboard enables healthcare management to monitor key performance indicators, identify trends, compare departments and patient segments, and make **data-driven business decisions**.
